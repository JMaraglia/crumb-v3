Privacy

Last updated November 13, 2026.

Join The Beta Form

When you join the beta on this website, we save your name, your email, your industry, your state, how you heard about Crumb and, if you give it, your company. We use them only to invite you to Crumb, to email you about the beta and to understand who is signing up. We don't sell or share them, and we don't add you to any other mailing list.

The Crumb App

Crumb keeps your customers, notes, photos and schedule on your phone. If you sign in to sync between your devices, that data is stored in Crumb's cloud database, where only your own account can read it. The Crumb team sees counts and dates to run the service (like how many devices are syncing), never the contents of your customers, notes or photos.

If you turn on reminders, the reminders for the next few days are stored on our server so they can be sent to your phone on time.

Your Choices

You can ask us to remove your beta sign-up or your Crumb account and its cloud data at any time, using Report A Problem in the Crumb app (Settings → Help).
