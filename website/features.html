Customers & Prospects

Every Account, Ready Before You Walk In.

Who they are, who to ask for, when you visit and who else they buy from.

  • Customers And Prospects In One Place

    Two lists that work the same way. Search by name, address or account number.

  • Every Contact, One Tap Away

    Call or text the owner, buyer or manager, open the address in Maps, or send an email.

  • Visit Schedules

    Set the day, the time and how often you go: weekly, every 2 or 3 weeks, monthly or every 2 months.

  • Competitor Tracking

    Note who else they buy from. Pick from a list or add your own.

  • Prospect Stages And Conversion

    Move a prospect from Cold to Ready To Close. When they sign, convert them to a customer and their notes, contacts and schedule come with them.

  • Groups For Multiple Locations

    One business with several locations stays together, whether each spot is a customer or a prospect.

Itinerary & Route Planning

Today's Route, Already Planned.

Open Crumb and your day is there: who to see, in what order and at what time.

  • Your Daily Visits

    Every stop and appointment for the day, with times and addresses.

  • Regular Visits Show Up On Their Own

    Accounts on a visit schedule land on the right days automatically.

  • Optimize Route

    One tap puts your flexible stops in the shortest driving order from where you are. Locked times and appointments stay put.

  • Check Off Stops

    Tap a stop when you're done. The Next Stop button opens the next one in Maps.

  • Move And Reschedule

    Hold and drag to change the order, or send a stop to tomorrow, next week or any day you pick. Wrap Up Today gathers what you didn't get to.

  • Due For A Check-In

    Accounts you haven't been in touch with for a while show up under today, one tap from your route. You pick how long "a while" is.

Calendar & Reminders

Appointments That Know Your Route.

A calendar built for the road, connected to your Itinerary.

  • Day, Week And Month

    Swipe through days and weeks. Month shows colored marks on each day; tap a day to see what's on it.

  • Quick Appointments

    Hold a thumb on an empty time or tap +. Pick a color, add the location and notes, and link it to a customer or prospect.

  • Connected To Your Itinerary

    Appointments linked to an account show on that day's Itinerary. Write a note, tap Meeting, and it's booked on both.

  • Your Regular Visits, Too

    Turn on Itinerary Visits to see your scheduled customer and prospect visits on the calendar.

  • Reminders

    A morning rundown of your stops, a heads-up before appointments, and an end-of-day list of what's still open. You choose the times.

On iPhone, reminders work when Crumb is opened from its Home Screen icon.

Notes & To-Dos

Promises Made On A Visit Don't Get Lost.

Every conversation lands on the right account, and every follow-up lands on your list.

  • Notes On Every Account

    Label a note Visit, Sample, Follow-up, Contacted or Background. Use the iPhone keyboard mic to talk instead of type.

  • Follow-Ups Become To-Dos

    Follow-up and Sample notes get a checkbox and a due date, and show up on your To-Do list automatically.

  • Every Note, Searchable

    The Notes screen holds every note from every account. Search the text, or filter by account, label, date or group.

  • Your Daily Tasks

    Add your own tasks with due dates. Overdue, Today and Upcoming, checked off in one place.

  • Share Or Print

    Send notes for today, this week or any date range as a PDF.

Call Sheet

Order Day, Covered.

Know who orders today, how to reach them and who you still haven't heard from.

  • Who Orders Today

    Customers grouped by order day, Sunday through Saturday, worked out from their delivery days.

  • Everything For The Call

    Account number, contact, phone, the best time to call, whether they order online, and your call note.

  • Check Off Orders

    Tick Ordered as each one comes in, with a running count like "3 of 6 ordered".

  • Fresh Every Order Day

    Checkmarks are saved by date, so the next order day starts clean.

  • Hand It Off

    Share the week as an Excel file with a tab for each day, or print it, so whoever covers for you knows who to call.
